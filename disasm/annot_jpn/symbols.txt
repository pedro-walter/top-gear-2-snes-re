;; Front-end (title / menus / car shop) entry: DBR=$9F, S=$1FFF, DP=$0100.  All menu code lives in bank 1F; it busy-waits on $4211/$4212.

;; brightness in $58/$5D (fixed-point 8.4 steps, limit $0F00) and bumps frame counters $1A/$1C.
# ---------------------------------------------------------------- decompression
